Welcome to Camp Tropical!

Where unforgettable adventures and lifelong memories have been in the making since 1985! Founded over four decades ago as a modest coastal retreat, our camp has grown into a premier destination dedicated to empowering young campers through immersive island exploration. Unlike traditional woodland camps, Camp Tropical takes full advantage of its breathtaking island setting and sun-drenched beaches. A tropical beach sleepaway camp offers kids a unique opportunity to unplug from screens, build independence, and forge strong friendships while falling asleep to the ocean breeze. Surrounded by white sands and turquoise waters, children engage in exciting water sports, marine discovery, and team-building beach activities that foster confidence, resilience, and a deep love for nature.
